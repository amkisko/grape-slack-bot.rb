## Decisions

- Cut 2.1.2 and raise the grape runtime ceiling to < 5.0.
- Relock grape to 4.0.0 in Gemfile.lock and appraisal lockfiles.
- Keep the live Grape request that calls slack_bot_config and asserts endpoint @config is not SlackBot::Config.
