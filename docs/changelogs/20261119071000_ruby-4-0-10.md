## Decisions

- Move the local MRI pin from 4.0.2 to 4.0.10.
- Pin CI MRI jobs to 4.0.10 and 3.4.13 so the 2026-11-18 security floors cannot slip on a minor-line resolver.
- Leave `gem.required_ruby_version = ">= 3"` unchanged.
- Leave RuboCop `TargetRubyVersion: 3.4` unchanged.
- Do not adopt Ruby 4.1.0-preview1.
- Skip a `CHANGELOG.md` Unreleased bullet: runtime pin and CI floors only; published gem contract unchanged.
